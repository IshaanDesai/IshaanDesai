## Welcome to my GitHub profile!

I am a scientific software developer focusing on developing methods, software, and tools for multiscale simulations. I am a part of the [preCICE project](https://github.com/precice) where I maintain and develop several repositories. My educational background is in mechanical engineering, scientific computing, and software engineering.

My most recent employment has been at the University of Stuttgart. There, I worked in the Cluster of Excellence SimTech, and my research was about developing adaptive and flexible software for two-scale coupled simulations (projects [PN5-9](https://www.simtech.uni-stuttgart.de/exc/research/pn/pn5/pn5-9/) and [PN5-9(II)](https://www.simtech.uni-stuttgart.de/exc/research/pn/pn5/pn5-9ii/)). During this work, I pursued a doctorate at the University of Stuttgart. I am currently seeking postdoctoral positions where I can apply my expertise in scientific computing and software development.

I am also interested in spreading Research Software Engineering practices. To this end, I have been part of curating and teaching the graduate-level courses [Simulation Software Engineering](https://simulation-software-engineering.github.io/homepage/) and [Research Software Engineering 102](https://github.com/RSE-102) taught at the University of Stuttgart.
