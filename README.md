## Welcome to my GitHub profile!

I am a scientific software developer focusing on developing methods and implementing software for coupled multiphysics and multiscale simulations. I am a part of the [preCICE project](https://github.com/precice) where I maintain several repositories. My educational background is in mechanical engineering, scientific computing, and software engineering. My most recent employment was at the University of Stuttgart. There, I worked in the Cluster of Excellence SimTech, and my research focused on developing adaptive, flexible software for multiscale coupled simulations. During this work, I pursued a doctorate at the University of Stuttgart. I am also interested in learning and spreading software sustainability practices. To this end, I have been part of the curation and teaching of the graduate-level courses [Simulation Software Engineering](https://simulation-software-engineering.github.io/homepage/) and [Research Software Engineering 102](https://github.com/RSE-102) at the University of Stuttgart.
